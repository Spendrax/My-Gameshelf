My Gameshelf — Privacy

My Gameshelf keeps your games, ratings, notes and settings only on your device (in the app's local storage). There is no account and nothing is sent to us.

When you search for a game, your search text is sent to the app's own lookup service (on Cloudflare), which asks IGDB (by Twitch) and the Steam store. Your search text and your own RAWG API key are sent to RAWG to find game info and pictures. Cover pictures are loaded from RAWG's servers.

If you turn on the live window weather, the area you choose (rounded, about a town) is sent to Open-Meteo to get the local weather. If you pick a city by name, that name is sent to Open-Meteo to find it.

Backups are files you save yourself; they stay wherever you put them.
